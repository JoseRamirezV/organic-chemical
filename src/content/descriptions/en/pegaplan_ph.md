# Pegaplan Ph

Contains 93% vegetable oil emulsifiable in water. It is indicated for use with insecticides, fungicides, herbicides, acaricides, and for controlling scale insects.

Benefits and Functions:

-Encapsulator: This property allows PEGAPLAN PH to form an oil film that adheres to the agrochemical molecule, protecting it from factors such as water pH, hardness, metals, and suspended solids.
-Adherent: This property is crucial for pest control.
