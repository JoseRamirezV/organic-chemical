# Nutriplan K45

It is a complex fertilizer for foliar or fertigation application, 100% soluble, chelated, and balanced with macro- and micronutrients. It can be applied at any crop growth stage and enhances vigor and vegetative growth. It is easily assimilated and transformed by plants.

APPLICATION METHOD AND TIMING

For foliar applications, it is essential to apply in early morning or late afternoon when leaves are turgid. Applications during midday, under high temperatures, or wilted plants may be ineffective. Use sufficient water volume for better results. It can also be applied aerially, alone or mixed with pesticides. Recommended for Avocado 🥑
