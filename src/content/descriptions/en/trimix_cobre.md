# Trimix Cobre

Fungicide
Classic bactericide with preventive action, broad activity spectrum, and good persistence.

TRIMIX COPPER

General information
Characteristics
Copper has fungistatic and bacteriostatic activity, inhibiting the vital functions of fungi and bacteria. In general, copper is strongly retained in the soil’s surface layer and is practically immobile. It has high affinity for soil colloids and forms stable complexes with organic compounds. Plants use it as a nutrient.
