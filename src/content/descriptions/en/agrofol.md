# Agrofol

It is a systemic fungicide from the phosphorous acid group that activates the plant’s natural defenses and combats diseases caused by Oomycete fungi such as Phytophthora and downy mildew. It is used preventively and for early curative control in crops like avocados, potatoes, grapes, and vegetables. 

**What is it used for?**
- Disease control: Effective against diseases caused by Oomycetes such as downy mildew and Phytophthora.
- Resistance inducer: Stimulates the plant’s natural defenses by activating systemic acquired resistance (SAR) responses. 
- Synergist: Its tank-mix compatibility with other fungicides enhances their effectiveness and residual activity. 
- Anti-stress: Helps crops recover from stress caused by herbicides, hail, or waterlogging. 

**Where is it used?**
- Fruit trees: Avocados and vines.
- Vegetables: Potatoes, tomatoes, asparagus, and more.
