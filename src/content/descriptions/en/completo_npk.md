# Completo Npk

INGENUITY AND TECHNOLOGY COMPLETE NPK PLANT NUTRITION Complete NPK Plant Nutrition is a combination of nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), and potassium (K) designed to maximize crop yield and quality. It is formulated to meet the specific needs of each crop. COMPLETE NPK is one of the most efficient sources of N, P, and K available on the market. Each prill of COMPLETE NPK contains balanced amounts of NPK precisely adapted to the crop’s requirements. When applied at the correct rate and under proper conditions, it promotes nutrient absorption. This product is a chemical complex and therefore provides uniform nutrient distribution, whether applied by broadcasting or with fertilizer spreaders. There is no risk of nutrient segregation during transport, handling, or application. COMPLETE NPK has a balanced nitrogen source that contains nitrogen in both nitrate and ammonium forms.
