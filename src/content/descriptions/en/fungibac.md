# Fungibac

It is a preventive and contact fungicide formulated with FUNGIBAC as the active ingredient, used to control fungal diseases in various crops such as avocado 🥑, potato, tomato, banana, and rice. It inhibits the cellular respiration of fungi, preventing spore germination and fungal entry into the plant.
Main characteristics
Type: Non-systemic, contact, and preventive fungicide.
Active ingredients:
OXYTETRACYCLINE AGRICULTURAL
PLUS
THIAZOLES
Formulation: Suspension concentrate (SC).
Mode of action: Blocks fungal respiration and protects the plant from infection.
