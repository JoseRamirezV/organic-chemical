# Cuaje Y Amarre

Its goal is to control excessive vigor by promoting the movement of assimilates from leaves to fruits and other storage organs. It enhances the mobilization of sugars and nutrients to key storage organs (roots, fruits, etc.), controls vegetative vigor, and reduces internode length. It concentrates and synchronizes flowering and fruit set, preventing fruit abortion and reabsorption. It promotes the growth of more compact plants with increased lateral development.
Presented as a soluble concentrate designed for foliar spraying.

Dosage:
Consult based on soil and crop analysis.

General conditions:
It is applied to most crops every 7 days; however, the frequency should not exceed 14 days.

If frequency increases to 14 days, the application dose should be 1 L/Ha.
