# Singer

Insecticide
Composition:
100 g/l Isocycloseram
150 g/l Lambda-cyhalothrin
Formulation:
A mixed CS and SC formulation (ZC)
Product description
SINGER®️ ZC is an insecticide formulated with Isocycloseram (PLINAZOLIN®️ or PLIN) and Lambda-cyhalothrin.
Isocycloseram (PLINAZOLIN®️ or PLIN) belongs to IRAC Group 30 insecticides by mode of action. It acts by contact and ingestion. It affects the nervous system as an allosteric modulator of the GABA-activated chloride channel. It binds to a novel site on GABA receptors in synapses and prevents them from opening. As a result, muscle relaxation fails, leading to continuous contractions and ultimately the insect’s death. Lambda-cyhalothrin (LCY) belongs to IRAC Group 3A. It penetrates the insect cuticle rapidly and disrupts nerve conduction by delaying sodium channel closure.
