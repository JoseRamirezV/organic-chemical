# Botrizan

It is an anilinopyrimidine fungicide used to control fungal diseases such as Botrytis cinerea (gray mold) and powdery mildew in fruits, vegetables, and ornamental plants. It acts both preventively and curatively by inhibiting methionine biosynthesis (a key amino acid for fungi) and the secretion of enzymes necessary for infection, thus blocking fungal penetration and mycelial development. It is a systemic fungicide with translaminar action, meaning it penetrates plant tissues and moves within them, providing leaf protection.

Mode of action: Methionine inhibition: BOTRIZAN blocks methionine production, an essential amino acid for protein and lipid synthesis in fungi, halting their development.

Enzymatic blockade: It also inhibits the secretion of hydrolytic enzymes fungi need to break down and penetrate plant tissues.

Uses and crops: Used to control diseases in various crops.
