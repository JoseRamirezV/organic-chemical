# Simpuk 75

It is a pyrethroid agricultural insecticide (Toxicological Category II – moderately hazardous) that acts by contact and ingestion, causing paralysis in insects by disrupting their nerve cell membranes. It is presented as an emulsifiable concentrate and is a product of Ingenio y Tecnología.
How it works
Neurotoxin: SIMPUK 75 acts as a neurotoxin, disrupting normal neuronal function.
Sodium ion alteration: Alters sodium ion flow through cell membranes, causing excessive nervous activity.
Paralysis: This repetitive nerve firing in sensory and motor pathways leads to paralysis and eventual death.
