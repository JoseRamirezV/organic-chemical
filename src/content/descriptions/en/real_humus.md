# Real Humus

It is a liquid organic amendment, formulated with top-quality raw materials using treated water that complies with quality standards, in line with the circular economy principles adopted by the company and in compliance with ICA requirements.

The use of REALHUMUS improves the physical, chemical, and microbiological characteristics of the soil, enhancing any type of crop. Among its main advantages are: pH regulation, increased cation exchange, restoration of biota, improving nutrient assimilation and root development, and enhancing the effectiveness of fertilizers, whether organic or chemical.
