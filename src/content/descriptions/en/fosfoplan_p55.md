# Fosfoplan P55

It is a concentrated, 100% water-soluble powder fertilizer designed to achieve and maintain the nutritional balance of most crops through its high nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium content. It also provides a balanced supply of micronutrients.

It is recommended to apply FOSFOPLAN P55®️ + MINORS to obtain and maintain a high nutritional level in crops during stages of high nutrient demand.

Benefits:
-Greater flower, fruit, and seed formation, increasing crop yield.
-Greater precocity.
-Increased synthesis of proteins and sugars.
-Enhanced root development. Recommended for Avocado 🥑
