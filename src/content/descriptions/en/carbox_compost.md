# Carbox Compost

Organic mineral fertilizer. Thanks to its composition, microbiological characteristics, and concentration of organic matter, it improves the physical and chemical conditions of various types of soil, enhancing their productivity and promoting their recovery. This product is made from 100% biodegradable organic matter, which is stabilized through a controlled process called composting. 

The constant application of CARBOX COMPOST increases the concentration of organic matter and the fixation of organic carbon, promoting soil nutrition, controlling erosion, and encouraging the recovery of its productive activity.
