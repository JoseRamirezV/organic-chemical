# Carbox Compound

Organic mineral fertilizer balanced between macro and microelements. Thanks to its composition, microbiological characteristics, concentration of organic matter, and concentration of major and minor elements, it favors the physical-chemical conditions of many types of soils, improving crop and soil productivity, promoting the recovery of poor and unstable soil layers, and serving as a source of nutrition for all types of crops. This product is made from a base of biodegradable organic matter, which is stabilized through a controlled process called composting and a mixture of nutrients rich in phosphorus, nitrogen, potassium, magnesium, and zinc. The constant application of CARBOX COMPOUND increases the concentration of organic matter and the fixation of organic carbon, promoting soil and crop nutrition, helping to control erosion, and encouraging the recovery of its productive activity.
