# Mas Llenado

As a growth regulator, it is designed to delay foliar senescence when plants lack a good root system. It increases the development of lateral shoots by inducing a phenomenon called “directed accumulation,” where the application of MAS LLENADO promotes the transport of essential substances to deficient areas, delaying senescence and actively participating in cell elongation.

TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Applied to plants under stress with high ethylene levels to stop premature fruit ripening. Improves fruit quality and shelf life. When applied to fruits between 2–8 mm in diameter, it stimulates cell division, resulting in higher-quality fruits. When applied frequently to roots, it strengthens the root system, providing greater resistance to pests and diseases.
